# The product photos rescued from Shopify

Every image the Shopify store had, downloaded before that store goes
away, with the product each one belonged to. They are in
`public/brand/designs/`.

These are the only design photographs that exist. The spreadsheets
have none — 7016 cells across the four files hold nothing but model
names, prices and YES/NO — so until new photography is done, this is
the whole library.

Sony, Nikon, Fujifilm, Sigma, Tamron, Samyang, Panasonic and Viltrox
Lens Skin. Those are the nine that actually had lens models and
prices behind them.

The handles `hhh`, `werdx`, `lenssss`, `cfnv` and `aaaaaaaaaaaaaaa`
are test products. Their photos are kept because a real photograph
sits behind two of them, not because the products matter.
